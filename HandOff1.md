## 3. Research Hypotheses

* **H1 (CNN Interaction):** The synergistic interaction between interleaved synaptic balancing and dropout generalizes to standard convolutional networks with shared weights on CIFAR-10.
* **H2 (Grid-Best Validation Dropout Rate):** Interleaved balancing shifts the best validation dropout probability within the tested discrete rate grid in a CNN, evaluated via orthogonal 1D validation sweeps over convolutional and fully connected dropout rates.
* **H3 (Generalization Across Complexity):** The interaction between dropout and synaptic balancing generalizes across increasingly complex image-classification tasks and produces reproducible improvements in convergence speed, generalization gap, or final classification accuracy relative to either method independently.

## 4. Staged Execution Plan & Statistical Protocol

1. **Stage 1 (Mathematical & Numerical Verification Gate):** Verify exact logit invariance in `float64` (`< 1e-12`) and `float32` (`< 1e-5`) across all 8 `(cost_domain, balance_mode, fc_balance_mode)` combinations (including active fixed-mask dropout), independent brute-force and `F.unfold` graph-cost verification (`< 1e-11` per layer, `< 1e-10` full network) with forward-pass output-shape checks, per-transition parameter-vs-graph multiplier divergence, zero-cost unit accounting, and paired augmentation/dropout RNG isolation (`--stage 1`).
2. **Stage 2 (CIFAR-10 $2 \times 2$ Factorial Experiment):**
   * **Exploratory Smoke (`--mode smoke`, `seed=42`, 5 epochs) & Validation Pilot (`--mode pilot`, `seeds=43–47`, 60 epochs):** Unlike the completed 5-seed MNIST confirmatory experiment, the 5-seed CIFAR-10 pilot is strictly a validation-only phase (`test_loader=None`). Use validation curves (`val_accuracy`, `val_loss`) and the unaugmented training-subset evaluation (`train_subset_eval_accuracy`) to verify whether 60 epochs of stateless SGD sufficiently train the CNN and to lock protocol settings before touching the test set.
   * **Confirmatory Run (`--mode full`, `seeds=48–55`, 60 epochs):** Unlock the official 10,000-image CIFAR-10 test set on 8 disjoint seeds after all architecture and optimizer settings are frozen. *(Statistical caveat: paired 95% confidence intervals and $p$-values across seeds quantify training, initialization, shuffling, and dropout randomness conditional on the fixed CIFAR-10 data split.)*
3. **Stage 3 (Mechanistic Controls & H2 Validation Sweep):** Compare `C_ref` vs. `C_rand` (Dropout Off) and `D_ref` vs. `D_rand` (Dropout On) under matched random function-preserving rescaling, reporting symmetric paired statistics (mean, SD, SE, 95% CI, $t$-statistic, and unadjusted $p$-value) for $\delta_C$, $\delta_D$, and $\delta_{\text{interaction}} = \delta_D - \delta_C$, alongside orthogonal 1D validation sweeps over dropout rates. Stage 3 withholds the official test set across all modes (`smoke`, `pilot`, and `full`) unless `--stage3-confirmatory-test-eval` is explicitly specified.
4. **Stage 4 (Architectural Scale-Up):** Extend to BatchNorm, residual skip connections, and CIFAR-100 / ImageNet subsets only after Stages 1–3 are established.
